Attackers begin moving laterally in as little as 27 seconds, yet it takes 247 days to identify and contain the average data breach. That gap is where business exposure lives – and it’s why boards and business leaders are increasingly focused on cyber resilience rather than reactive detection and response. Instead of measuring alert volume or response times, security leaders need a defensible answer to the question: if a security breach occurs tomorrow, will the business keep running?…
