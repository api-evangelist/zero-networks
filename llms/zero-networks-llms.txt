# Zero Networks

> Zero Networks helps organizations prevent attacks, minimize blast radius, and maintain business continuity – even when attackers get inside.

## Platform
- [The Zero Networks Platform](https://zeronetworks.com/platform): Fully automated for Zero Trust segmentation and remote access
- [Network Segmentation](https://zeronetworks.com/platform/network-segmentation): Radically simple segmentation in a click
- [Identity Segmentation](https://zeronetworks.com/platform/identity-segmentation): Restrict admin and service account access to operational needs only
- [Secure Remote Access](https://zeronetworks.com/platform/secure-remote-access): The speed of VPN and the security of ZTNA combined
- [AI Segmentation](https://zeronetworks.com/platform/ai-capabilities): Stop AI lateral movement automatically
- [Network Map](https://zeronetworks.com/platform/network-map): Network Map shows you what’s happening live across every asset, identity, and environment
- [Integrations](https://zeronetworks.com/platform/integrations)

## Use Cases
- [Block Ransomware](https://zeronetworks.com/use-cases/block-ransomware): Lock down lateral movement and leave hackers stranded with multi-factor segmentation
- [Protect Service Accounts](https://zeronetworks.com/use-cases/discover-and-protect-service-accounts): Prevent service account abuse and lateral movement by automatically restricting logon rights to operational needs only
- [Enforce MFA on Admin Accounts](https://zeronetworks.com/use-cases/restrict-admin-accounts): Easily monitor and control admin logons to temporarily allow privileged access after multi-factor authentication
- [Apply MFA to Anything](https://zeronetworks.com/use-cases/apply-mfa-to-anything): Implement automated, agentless, MFA-powered microsegmentation
- [Segment Every Asset](https://zeronetworks.com/use-cases/segmenting-every-asset): Zero Networks restricts access to essential assets only and applies just-in-time MFA
- [Safeguard Domain Controllers](https://zeronetworks.com/use-cases/enhance-domain-controller-security): Safeguard Domain Controllers against unauthorized access and potential cyber threats – without interrupting operations – with RPC Firewall
- [Replace VPN with ZTNA](https://zeronetworks.com/use-cases/replace-vpn-with-ztna): Connect remote employees and third parties to the network with zero trust principles and zero performance impact
- [Connect Remote Users](https://zeronetworks.com/use-cases/connect-remote-users): Combine the speed of VPN and the security of ZTNA – without their flaws
- [Protect Third Party Access](https://zeronetworks.com/use-cases/limit-3rd-party-access): Seamlessly mitigate security, data privacy, compliance, and regulatory concerns when connecting external entities to the organization
- [Pass a Pen Test](https://zeronetworks.com/use-cases/pen-test): Zero Networks leverages automated segmentation and MFA to restrict excessive network and privileged permissions, the root cause of most successful attacks (and failed pen tests)
- [Comply with Cyber Insurance](https://zeronetworks.com/use-cases/comply-with-cyber-insurance): Boost your audit scores, pass every pen test, and check the compliance boxes
- [Reduce Security OpEx](https://zeronetworks.com/use-cases/reduce-security-opex): Zero Networks cuts the costs associated with long deployment times, manual rule creation, ongoing maintenance, and more
